# Reader appearance becomes three named presets, not binary light/dark

The reader currently has one appearance axis: `next-themes`' binary `light`/`dark`, toggled via `ThemeToggle.jsx` and resolved in `chakra.jsx` through Chakra's built-in `_light`/`_dark` semantic-token condition. We're replacing this with three named presets - `paper` (the current look, kept as the default), `night` (near-black, warm low-blue-light accent, for low-light listening), and `soft` (a cooler, softer-shadow daytime alternative) - selectable from the player's new settings sheet.

Persistence and no-flash-on-load stay exactly where ADR 0001 put them: owned entirely by `next-themes` (its own `localStorage` key, its own blocking inline script), not folded into `listenerSettings.js`. `next-themes` already supports an arbitrary list of named themes via its `themes` prop, not just `light`/`dark`, so `ColorModeProvider` only needs `themes={['paper', 'night', 'soft']}` and a matching `defaultTheme`; the flash-of-wrong-theme guarantee ADR 0001 called out is unaffected.

**The wrinkle: Chakra's `_light`/`_dark` shorthand can't express a third state.** Per `colorMode.jsx`'s existing comment, that shorthand isn't a generic light/dark toggle - it's hardcoded (in `@chakra-ui/react`'s preset-base.js) to resolve against whether the `.dark` class is present on `<html>`. It has no notion of a third value. `chakra.jsx` therefore needs its own custom conditions - `_paper`, `_night`, `_soft`, mapped to `&:where(.paper)`, `&:where(.night)`, `&:where(.soft)` via `createSystem`'s `conditions` config - and every existing semantic token (`background`, `foreground`, `accent`, `danger`, `activeSentenceBg`, `activeSentenceFg`, plus whatever the scroll-position fill and settings-sheet surface end up needing) moves from `{ _light, _dark }` to `{ _paper, _night, _soft }`. `next-themes`' `attribute="class"` stays as-is; it just applies `paper`/`night`/`soft` as the class name instead of `light`/`dark`.

This ADR only concerns the theme axis. Voice and playback speed are unaffected and remain in `listenerSettings.js`.

Considered alternatives: keeping binary light/dark and just reskinning dark mode with a warmer palette (rejected - conflates "low light" with "an alternate light-mode aesthetic," which are different axes the design review wanted to keep independently choosable); storing the theme choice in `listenerSettings.js` alongside voice/speed (rejected - reopens the flash-of-wrong-theme problem ADR 0001 already solved, since `next-themes`' blocking script only knows about its own storage key); keeping Chakra's `_light`/`_dark` shorthand and treating `soft` as a light variant and `night` as the dark one (rejected - collapses `paper` and `soft`, two visually distinct light-ish themes, onto the same condition, losing the ability to pick between them independently of the day/night axis).
